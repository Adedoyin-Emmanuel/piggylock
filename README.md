# PiggyLock

PiggyLock is a savings app for MiniPay. You put money aside until a date you choose, and nobody can touch it before then, not even you.

## How it works

1. **Create a vault.** Give it a name (like "School fees"), choose an amount, and pick how long to lock it for.
2. **Wait.** Your savings stay locked, and you can see exactly how long is left.
3. **Withdraw.** When the date arrives, take your money back with one tap.

## Features

- Lock USDT for a week, a month, a few months or a year
- Keep several vaults at once, each with its own goal and date
- Live countdown for every vault
- See your total savings at a glance
- Light and dark mode

## Fees

A **0.25%** fee is taken when you withdraw. For example, locking 100 USDT gets you 99.75 USDT back. The fee is shown before you confirm, and it can never change.

## Is my money safe?

- Your savings are held by a smart contract on the Celo blockchain, not by PiggyLock.
- No one can withdraw early. There is no admin, no override and no back door.
- Only you can withdraw your vault, and only after the date you chose.

> PiggyLock is an early prototype running on a test network. It hasn't been audited yet, so please don't use real money.
